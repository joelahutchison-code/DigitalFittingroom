          About this prototype

          What runs live

          Three AI models. A try-on model generates a new image of you wearing the piece. An image-editing model redraws that image looser or tighter when you pick another size. A video model turns a look into a short runway clip. Everything you make is kept in this browser, so it reopens at once.

          What is simulated

          Stock, prices, delivery, fit data and return-risk figures are invented to show the idea. The stylist follows written rules where a real agent would reason with a language model over Next's live catalogue, stock and order systems. Size images are AI illustrations of looser and tighter fits. They are not measured from the garment.

          Your photo

          The photo and the images made from it go from this browser to public AI demo servers on Hugging Face and nowhere else. This page has no server of its own. Results are stored only in this browser, and Remove photo deletes them. Saved and shared images carry an AI-generated label.
