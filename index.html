      About this prototype

      What runs live

      A virtual try-on AI model takes your photo and the photo of the piece and generates a new image of you wearing it, keeping your face, pose and background. Each image takes about half a minute. Images you have made are kept in this browser, so they reopen at once.

      What it cannot do yet

      The image shows cut, colour and drape on your body, but it does not change with the size you pick: the fit notes carry the size logic. Details the product photo does not show are guessed. The free model has a daily limit and can be busy. A retailer would run its own model on its own product, size and returns data.

      Your photo

      The photo goes from this browser to the model's public demo server on Hugging Face and nowhere else. This page has no server of its own. Generated images are stored only in this browser, and Remove photo clears them.
