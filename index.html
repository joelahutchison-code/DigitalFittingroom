                About this prototype

                What runs live

                Four AI models. A try-on model generates a new image of you wearing the piece. An image-editing model redraws that image looser or tighter when you pick another size. A video model turns a look into a short runway clip. A small image-and-text model reads your style photos inside this browser and scores them against four styles. Everything you make is kept in this browser, so it reopens at once.

                What is simulated

                Stock, prices, delivery, fit data and return-risk figures are invented to show the idea. The stylist follows written rules where a real agent would reason with a language model over Next's live catalogue, stock and order systems. Each piece is tagged by hand with the styles it suits. A retailer would learn that from what shoppers buy, keep and send back. Size images are AI illustrations of looser and tighter fits. They are not measured from the garment.

                Your photo

                The photo and the images made from it go from this browser to public AI demo servers on Hugging Face and nowhere else. This page has no server of its own. Results are stored only in this browser, and Remove photo deletes them. Saved and shared images carry an AI-generated label. Style photos never leave this browser: the model that reads them is downloaded once and runs on your device, and only the four style scores are kept.
